UK AI Governance — Built In, Not Bolted On
Every architecture decision treats the FCA AI Principles, ICO UK GDPR guidance, and the UK AI Safety Institute Responsible AI framework as non-negotiable design constraints — not afterthoughts.
UK Regulatory Requirement
How We Deliver It
Architecture Implication
F
Fairness
UK Regulatory Requirement
The FCA Principles for Businesses (Principle 6) and Consumer Duty (2023) require AI-driven placement, claims, and servicing decisions do not produce discriminatory outcomes. Models must be auditable for bias across client segments, risk categories, and geographic regions.
How We Deliver It
AI recommendations across placement, claims advocacy, and client servicing are independently auditable. Decisions are verifiable independently of financial records. Placement and claims outcomes governed consistently — no client segment receives systematically different treatment.
Architecture Implication
Rules engine as independent API — consistent decision logic across Prospecting, Placement, Servicing, and Claims domains
E
Ethics
UK Regulatory Requirement
The FCA Consumer Duty and ICO UK GDPR Accountability Principle require human accountability for all AI-influenced claim repudiations, placement declinatures, and coverage decisions. AI cannot replace human oversight on regulated decisions affecting clients.
How We Deliver It
Human override is possible at any point without stopping transaction processing. Kill-switch on AI agents does not halt workflow. All AI outputs are non-binding recommendations — claims advocates and placement directors retain final authority on regulated decisions.
Architecture Implication
AI kill-switch decoupled from workflow execution — AI can be suspended, brokerage operations continue without interruption
A
Accountability
UK Regulatory Requirement
FCA SM&CR and the ICO Accountability Framework require a clear chain of responsibility for every AI decision — from model owner to Senior Manager. Audit trails must be producible for FCA inspection within 24 hours.
How We Deliver It
One system is accountable for each decision type. Process orchestration owns workflow accountability. Placement system owns financial accountability. AI Hub owns AI reasoning accountability. The FCA receives a single, clean answer with full trace to a named Senior Manager.
Architecture Implication
One system of record per function — no competing accountability claims across platforms or vendor boundaries
T
Transparency
UK Regulatory Requirement
FCA Consumer Duty (Outcome 4) and ICO Transparency guidance require that placement declinatures and claims repudiations explain the basis of the decision in plain language. AI-influenced decisions must be explainable to both the FCA and to clients.
How We Deliver It
The reasoning trace and financial posting are separately queryable. AI Hub logs every agent decision with reasoning steps, evidence cited, and confidence score. Explanations to clients generated from the same reasoning chain — not post-hoc construction.
Architecture Implication
Unified audit service aggregates all system logs — reconstructs any decision for FCA inspection or FOS complaint response
S
Safety
UK Regulatory Requirement
The UK AI Safety Institute Responsible AI Framework and NCSC Cyber Security guidance require AI systems in regulated financial services are robust, monitored for drift, and protected against adversarial manipulation or unintended systemic risk.
How We Deliver It
Every agent has a defined confidence threshold; outputs below threshold route to human review. Model drift monitoring triggers review before incorrect decisions accumulate. Adversarial input detection at the LLM gateway layer. Howden holds an independent record of all AI decisions.
Architecture Implication
Confidence-gated HITL enforcement — no AI decision below threshold reaches a client without human ratification
FCA Consumer Duty (2023)
Agent Registry satisfies FCA model inventory requirement. Full agent lifecycle controls — versioning, rollback, deprecation. Board-level AI oversight documented under SM&CR.
ICO UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018
Automated decision-making rights (Article 22 UK GDPR): every AI-influenced decision is flagged; human review available on request; explanation generated from reasoning chain.
FCA SM&CR Accountability
Named Senior Manager accountable for each AI domain. Accountability map maintained in AI Governance namespace. FCA inspection package auto-assembled from audit vault within 24 hours.
UK AI Safety Institute Framework
Responsible AI evaluation conducted pre-deployment. Frontier model usage governed by UKASI guidelines. Red-team adversarial testing documented. AI incident reporting aligned to NCSC guidance.
Governance Architecture — Evidence Against FCA, ICO & UKASI
Model Governance & Unapproved Model Blocking
Model cards in AI Governance namespace: training data, known limitations, fairness assessment, approved use cases
Agent Registry blocks unapproved models from production — no shadow AI across brokerage operations
Versioning enforced: every model change triggers full governance review before deployment
Board-level AI oversight: CISO and Chief Risk Officer sign off all Tier 1 agent deployments
Routing Governance & Audit Mechanisms
LLM gateway routes by task type, cost tier, latency, and data sensitivity class — policy-driven, not ad-hoc
OpenTelemetry spans cover every routing event: agent execution, LLM call, knowledge retrieval, rules evaluation
Rate limiting per agent identity; token tracking per workflow, LOB, and department
Vendor AI subject to same audit sink requirement — Howden holds independent record of all AI decisions
Bias Monitoring & AML / FCA Screening
Fairness metrics computed on all agent outputs — demographic segmentation by client type, product, sector, and region
Real-time bias monitoring dashboard for Compliance team — configurable alert thresholds aligned to FCA Equality Act guidance
AML/CTF: all high-value transactions screened against HMT sanctions lists and FATF grey/black lists
Drift detection: confidence score distribution shifts trigger review before incorrect decisions accumulate
Tamper-Proof Audit Logs (Cryptographically Chained)
Audit event stream: indefinite retention, immutable — events cannot be removed; meets FCA 6-year record-keeping requirement
SHA-256 cryptographic hash chain — tampering produces INTEGRITY_FAILURE detectable by FCA examiner
Object Lock COMPLIANCE mode — immutable at storage layer; UK data residency enforced
Audit assembly: given any trace ID, retrieves complete package, verifies hash chain, exports for FCA or FOS
All five governance controls are architectural properties — not policies. They apply to every decision across every vendor boundary and cannot be bypassed at the application layer. FCA inspection readiness is a platform guarantee, not a project task.
